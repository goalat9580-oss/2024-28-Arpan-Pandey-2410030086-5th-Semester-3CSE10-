## 🌾 Problem Statement

Agribusiness generates large amounts of information related to **crop production, weather conditions, market prices, and business performance**. Raw agricultural data can contain missing values, inconsistencies, and errors, making direct analysis difficult.

The internship addressed this challenge by developing a structured approach for **collecting, cleaning, visualizing, forecasting, and evaluating agribusiness data** to support better data-driven decision-making.
